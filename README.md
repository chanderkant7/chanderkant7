# 💫 About Me:
Data & AI Consultant specializing in AI/ML platforms, distributed data systems, and cloud architecture, with 25+ years of experience designing and scaling enterprise data and AI solutions. Currently serving as Director of Architecture at Optum, I lead enterprise Lakehouse architecture and governance, enabling secure, scalable, and compliant data platforms in regulated healthcare environments. <br><br>My expertise spans data engineering, machine learning platforms, and Generative AI systems, with a strong focus on building production-grade AI solutions across AWS and Azure. I have deep experience in AI/ML lifecycle management using MLflow and implementing structured AI SDLC practices through spec-driven development (Spec-Kit approach), ensuring reproducibility, governance, and scalable deployment. <br><br>I actively work with AI-driven engineering practices, integrating LLM capabilities into enterprise systems using technologies from OpenAI and Anthropic, including Codex-style code generation, Claude-based reasoning assistants, and natural language-driven data interaction. My work increasingly focuses on designing agentic AI workflows that enable intelligent automation of data and engineering processes. <br><br>Over the years, I have led the design and delivery of large-scale data platforms processing hundreds of terabytes, supporting real-time analytics and operational intelligence, while also building high-performing teams, mentoring architects, and establishing governance frameworks that ensure reliability, security, and long-term scalability.
